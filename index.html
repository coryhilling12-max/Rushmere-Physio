How does physiotherapy work?

1

We find out what's wrong

We start by talking — what's the main issue, how long it's been going on, and what you want to get back to. That alone often points to the problem.

2

We look at your movement

Simple movements confirm what we think and add detail. Seeing how you've adapted to the issue is key to fixing it.

3

We guide you through the fix

Targeted hands-on treatment, specific exercises and clear advice. Most people notice changes within the first session.
